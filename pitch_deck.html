Explainable Street-Level AQI

Environmental Hacks (DTU) • Air track • AWS-first

Team: Shourya Sankrit

Goal: Democratize air quality data by moving beyond zip codes, using Machine Learning to interpolate PM2.5 to any street corner, and explaining why in plain English.

The Problem

  • Sparse hardware: Delhi has millions of residents but only a few dozen official CPCB stations.
  • Black box numbers: Standard apps say "AQI 300" but never explain what is causing it.
  • Static reporting: Users can't check the air quality of their specific commute route or neighborhood.
🌫️ 📉 🛑

The Solution

  • Click anywhere: Get estimated PM2.5 for exact map coordinates using spatial interpolation.
  • XGBoost + SHAP: A machine learning model trained on historical weather and OpenAQ data.
  • Generative AI: AWS Bedrock (Claude 3 Haiku) translates raw SHAP feature importance into plain English advice.
Input: [28.61, 77.20] ↓ Fetch Weather (Temp, Wind, BLH) Fetch Spatial (Road density) ↓ XGBoost predicts: 145 µg/m³ SHAP extracts: [Month, Wind] ↓ Bedrock: "High pollution due to winter inversion. Wear an N95."
